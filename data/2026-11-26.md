**원문 초록 (Abstract)**: A protein can engage multiple partners, making it challenging to disrupt disease-associated interactions while preserving others. We developed Bivexia, an AI agent that guides a multistage pipeline from discovery and de novo design through preclinical evaluation. Its decision rules are fixed in advance, and each prospective selection by the agent is cryptographically committed before testing. The objective is to discover small molecules that disrupt specified interactions of a target protein while preserving others. A compound advanced by Bivexia bound LAG-3 with nanomolar affinity and disrupted its interactions with tau and amyloid-β while preserving binding to MHC class II and FGL1. It reduced tau and amyloid-β burden in independent cohorts across two mouse models. The same rules gave three of nine hits on PrPᶜ and two of nine on LRP1, none in random controls. Our findings establish a framework for AI agents to pursue defined interaction selectivity across targets.
